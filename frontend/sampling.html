What is Monetary Unit Sampling?

Monetary Unit Sampling (MUS) is a statistical sampling method used in auditing to test the accuracy of financial records. Instead of treating each transaction as a sampling unit, MUS treats every individual currency unit (e.g., one MAD) as a unit.

This means larger transactions have a proportionally higher chance of being selected for audit review, effectively directing the auditor's attention to the highest financial risks in the population.
